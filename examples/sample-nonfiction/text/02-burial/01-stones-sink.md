If worms bring earth up, what lies on the surface goes down. Darwin found cinders and chalk spread on fields years before lying inches beneath the grass, and the floors of Roman buildings sunk under the turf. [Worms bury what lies on the ground](#clm_b4ry50): the slow [burial](#con_b4r1a0) of everything left on a field.
