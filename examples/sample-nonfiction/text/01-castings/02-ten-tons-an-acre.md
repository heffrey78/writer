[Charles Darwin](#per_darw1n) took up the question in his last book, [The Formation of Vegetable Mould through the Action of Worms](#src_d4rw1n), in 1881. He weighed the [castings](#con_ca5t50) worms left on the surface, plot by plot, and reckoned that in many parts of England more than ten tons of dry earth passed through their bodies on each acre every year.[^1]

From that he drew his claim: [worms make the topsoil](#clm_t0p501). Every particle of the mould, he thought, had passed through a worm many times over.

[^1]: [Vegetable Mould](#src_d4rw1n). He gives the figure in metric too: 10,516 kilogrammes.
