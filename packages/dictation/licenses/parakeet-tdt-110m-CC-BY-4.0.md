# Parakeet TDT 110M (English)

Writer's dictation settles each phrase with NVIDIA's Parakeet TDT 110M speech recognition model (`nvidia/parakeet-tdt_ctc-110m`), https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt_ctc-110m, by NVIDIA Corporation.

It's licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

Changes: the model was exported to ONNX and quantized to 8-bit integers by the sherpa-onnx project (`sherpa-onnx-nemo-parakeet_tdt_transducer_110m-en-36000-int8`), https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx. Writer downloads that export as it is, and changes nothing in it.
